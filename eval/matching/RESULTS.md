# Matching eval (v6 step 8)

Generated by `npm run eval:matching` (scripts/eval/matching/run.ts). TREC Clinical Trials cancer subset, human qrels (0 not relevant, 1 excluded, 2 eligible), hand-written structured answers per topic. Dev = TREC 2021 (10 topics), test = TREC 2022 (2 topics). Means over topics with bootstrap 95% CIs (4000 resamples over topics). Not a clinical-accuracy claim.

Arms: **baseline** = v5 pipeline order; **resolver** = the top 15 of that order (what rankTrials returns and find-flow reorders) re-ranked by deterministic per-criterion labels (Tier A first, then incScore), rest unchanged; **llm** = resolver + Groq judge on the unknown items of the top 10 (MATCH_JUDGE.topN); **resolver, full pool** = exploratory, the resolver tiering over every candidate (not what the app does).

Caveats: 10 dev topics and 2 test topics are very few; the test CIs (bootstrap over 2 topics) are not meaningful. The candidate pool is each topic's judged trials (the TREC pool), not the national index. Groq free tier: requests are capped below the production batch size (see LLM judge notes).

Definitions: P@10 = share of the top 10 judged eligible (2). Missed exclusions = share of the top 10 that TREC judged excluded (1) and the arm still shows in Tier A (baseline has no tiers, so all shown count). Unknown share = unknown labels / all criterion labels of the top-10 trials.
